Why haven't you started with crypto?

For most people it isn't a lack of interest. It's too many decisions.

How much should I start with?

A lump sum feels like too much, and a small amount feels pointless.

Which coin should I buy?

There are hundreds of coins, and the news you notice is usually old news.

Isn't this the top?

Waiting for the right moment turns into waiting, and a year goes by.

Tikkle takes all three decisions off your plate. The amount builds up from spare change as you spend, the app picks and proposes the coin, and you only decide whether to approve.

Three steps and you're set

Connect once. After that you only review and approve.

1

Connect your Kbank card

Register the last four digits of your Kbank card and choose how spare change is rounded. You can change the rule any time.

You also connect your Upbit Open API key and a two-factor method at this step. The app walks you through issuing the key.

2

Pay as usual, spare change adds up

Pay like you always do and spare change accrues by your rule. You can review and edit every entry in the app.

3

Approve, and it's bought

When spare change comes in, the app proposes which coin to buy and how much. Review, approve, and it's bought on Upbit.

What we read, and why

Money is involved, so we say this up front.

Notification access

To calculate spare change we read the amount and merchant name from Kbank card payment notifications. That permission is used for nothing else.

What we never read

We never ask for or store card PINs, account passwords or digital certificates. Notifications from other apps are not processed.

Pause and disconnect

You can pause reading notifications at any time and resume when you like. You can disconnect your card or delete your account, and deleting the account deletes the collected payment history with it.

Crypto assets can lose their entire value and are highly volatile. Tikkle does not take responsibility for investment decisions. Every investment decision is your own.
